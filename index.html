ii. What goes on the table

Four ways to feed a room.

  1. 01

    Grazing tables

    A whole counter turned into a feast. Tiered wooden boards of cheese and charcuterie, grapes, olives, chutneys and little pots of honey, twisted breadsticks, sandwiches, sausage rolls and pastries from the bakery.

    18ths · 40ths · Birthdays · Celebrations

  2. 02

    Themed tables

    Give us a theme and we run with it. For one Halloween party that meant skeleton props, mummy brownies and monster cupcakes, with the charcuterie on theme too.

    Halloween · Hen dos · Kids' parties

  3. 03

    Grazing boards & boxes

    The same idea, made smaller. Boards for the middle of the table, and boxes to carry to a friend's: one customer took a sweet grazing box to a Christmas catch-up.

    Smaller gatherings

  4. 04

    The bakery

    Fresh bread, pastries, homemade cakes, sandwiches and coffee, at the shop on Rusthall High Street. Neighbours named it a Nextdoor Neighbourhood Favourite in 2023.

    Every day · 9 am–5 pm

v. Our story

Our dad's bakery. Our tables.

Manuel Carvalho opened the bakery in September 2009. Today it is run by his daughters, Maria and Alex.

The tables began with a hen do. In April 2023 we made a grazing table for a sister's, and it became a business of its own. That autumn we closed our St John's Road shop after 13 years, to give our time to Rusthall High Street and to the tables.
